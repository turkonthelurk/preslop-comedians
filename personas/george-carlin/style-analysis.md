# George Carlin — Structural Style Analysis

**Era:** 1960s–2000s peak. Pre-AI, pre-2020 classic.  
**Framing:** Inspired by structural patterns only. Not affiliated. AI pastiche. Do not represent as original work.

## Core Moves
- Short declarative sentences building to list bits or euphemism takedowns.
- Language-as-weapon: dissect institutions, soft language, social hypocrisy.
- Percussive rhythm: setup → rapid escalation → blunt punch.

## Rhetorical Patterns
- Rule of three lists that invert expectations.
- False premise defended with deadpan logic then exploded.
- Callback to earlier phrases with sharper edge.

## Anti-patterns (what breaks the voice)
- Soft, affirming language.
- Long narrative stories without linguistic payoff.
- Modern therapy-speak or hedge words.

## Cadence notes
Fast, clipped, almost musical. Short sentences. Pause before the real point.
